 E-Commerce Funnel & Retention Analysis

Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing e-commerce customer behaviour using SQL to understand conversion performance, purchasing patterns, and customer retention.

The analysis examines the customer journey from product views to completed purchases, identifies major funnel drop-off points, and evaluates customer retention through cohort and RFM analysis.

 Business Objectives
- Analyse the e-commerce conversion funnel across different purchase stages.
- Identify the stages with the highest customer drop-off.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour and repeat-purchase patterns.
- Measure customer retention and 90-day churn.
- Compare conversion and retention performance across marketing channels.
- Generate actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.

 Tools & Technologies
- **SQL (MySQL): Data extraction, cleaning, transformation, joins, aggregations, CTEs, and analytical queries.
- **Excel:** Dataset inspection and preliminary data validation.

Dataset Overview
The dataset contains approximately 120,000 e-commerce records and includes information related to:
- Customer activity and purchasing behaviour
- Product views and shopping cart activity
- Checkout and purchase completion
- Marketing acquisition channels
- Customer and transaction details
- Purchase dates and revenue

 Analysis Performed

 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Inspected the dataset for missing and inconsistent values.
- Standardised date formats and relevant column names.
- Validated customer and transaction information.
- Prepared the data for analytical queries.

 2. E-Commerce Funnel Analysis
Analysed the customer journey through four stages:

View → Cart → Checkout → Purchase**

- Calculated stage-wise conversion rates.
- Measured customer drop-offs between funnel stages.
- Identified potential friction points in the purchase journey.

 3. Customer Retention & Cohort Analysis
- Grouped customers based on their first purchase month.
- Analysed customer activity across subsequent months.
- Evaluated retention patterns over time.
- Examined repeat-purchase behaviour.

 4. RFM Analysis
Applied RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to understand customer value and purchasing behaviour.

- **Recency:** How recently a customer made a purchase.
- **Frequency:** How often a customer purchased.
- **Monetary:** How much a customer spent.

5. Customer Churn & Channel Analysis
- Evaluated 90-day customer churn.
- Analysed repeat-purchase rates.
- Compared customer behaviour across Organic, Paid Ads, Social, and Email channels.

 Key Findings

- Analysed approximately 120,000 e-commerce records.
- Identified 8,181 completed purchases.
- Calculated an overall purchase conversion rate of approximately 6.82%.
- Identified Paid Ads as the highest contributor to purchase volume.
- Email recorded the highest channel-level conversion rate at approximately 7.31%.
- Observed a repeat-purchase rate of approximately 0.52% among purchasing customers.
